TWO FOUNDERS. ONE PASSION.

MORE THAN A RIDING GROUP

IT STARTED
WITH A RIDE.

The Send Club Offroading grew from a simple idea between friends into a riding group and content platform. What began with a love for four-wheelers and dirt bikes became a place to bring riders together, share the adventures, and build a community.

01 / FOUNDER & OWNER

HUNTER

Hunter's riding story began at age four on a 50cc four-wheeler. From a Polaris 110 to a Honda CRF100F and a Kawasaki KX250F, he grew into motocross and hopes to start racing soon. He helped turn the original riding-group idea into The Send Club.

02 / FOUNDER & OWNER

MIKEY

Mikey met Hunter in 2020 and found his passion for dirt bikes toward the end of 2023, after Hunter introduced him to riding. With a background on four-wheelers, he joined Hunter in building The Send Club into a community for riders and content fans.
